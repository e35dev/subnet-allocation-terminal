# Subnet Allocation Terminal

A professional allocation cockpit for Bittensor staking that helps capital allocators decide which subnets deserve stake, execute allocations inside the product, and continuously monitor every position without switching tools.

## Who it serves
Capital allocators deciding where to stake TAO across Bittensor subnets, Portfolio operators who need a configurable terminal for monitoring subnet exposure, performance, and risk, Newer allocators who want smart default layouts before building a custom workspace

## Optimize toward
An allocator can start from a simple default workspace, understand which Bittensor subnets are most attractive for staking, act on that decision in the same product, and keep monitoring a comprehensive but curated view of their allocations over time.

## Accept work that
- Open the terminal to a default allocator dashboard showing current portfolio allocation, ranked subnet opportunities, key performance and risk signals, recent changes, and immediate action paths.
- Compare subnets using allocator-relevant signals such as performance over time, emissions, stake dynamics, validator behavior, liquidity or capacity constraints, risk indicators, and subnet fundamentals.
- Inspect a subnet from the ranking view to understand why it may deserve more, less, or no stake, with charts, signal explanations, historical context, and comparable alternatives.
- Allocate or rebalance stake directly from the platform after reviewing the decision context, with a clear preview of intended changes before confirmation.
- Monitor all active allocations in one place, including exposure, performance, changes over time, risk alerts, and which positions may need attention.
- Customize the workspace like a professional terminal by choosing modules, saved layouts, watchlists, rankings, charts, tables, and monitoring panels, while keeping useful default layouts available.

## Close work that
- Do not build a generic block explorer centered on raw chain browsing.
- Do not make allocation a separate off-platform step.
- Do not bury the portfolio view behind subnet research pages.
- Do not expose internal platform mechanics, provider names, repository operations, labels, or maintainer concepts in customer-facing surfaces.
- Do not make customization mandatory before the product becomes useful.

## Keep it feeling
- Professional trading-terminal feel with dense, scannable information
- Simple first-run default layouts that do not require configuration to be useful
- Highly configurable workspace for advanced allocators
- Clear rankings, tables, charts, watchlists, and allocation controls
- Comprehensive data surface presented through curated modules rather than overwhelming raw feeds
